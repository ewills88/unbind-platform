# Unbind Pricing

## Standard Plan — $179/month
The standard Unbind subscription includes:
- Unlimited cases
- AI document categorization
- Discovery management
- Settlement tools & MSA generation
- Billing & invoicing with Stripe payments
- Trust accounting (IOLTA compliant)
- Client portal with magic link access
- E-filing integration (Tyler/Odyssey)
- Analytics & reporting with export
- Google Calendar sync
- Webhook integrations
- Email notifications
- All 50 state support

## Beta Pricing — $89/month (Locked for Life)
Early adopters who sign up during our limited beta period get:
- All features included in the standard plan
- $89/month rate locked for life — your price never increases
- Priority access to new features
- Direct line to the founding team for feedback
- This rate is available for a limited time only

## Enterprise
For firms with multiple attorneys:
- No per-seat charges — add your whole team
- Custom onboarding and training
- Dedicated account manager
- Custom integrations
- Priority support
- Contact sales@unbind.law for pricing

## Free Trial
- 14-day free trial with full access to all features
- No credit card required to start
- All data preserved if you subscribe after trial

## Payment
- Monthly billing via Stripe
- Cancel anytime — no long-term contracts
- Invoices and receipts available in your account settings

## What's NOT Included
- Court filing fees (passed through at cost)
- Stripe payment processing fees (standard Stripe rates apply to client payments)
- Custom development or integrations beyond what's offered

## FAQ
**Is there a setup fee?** No. Sign up and start using Unbind immediately.

**Can I switch plans?** Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade at any time. Changes take effect on your next billing cycle.

**Do you offer annual billing?** Yes, annual billing is available at a discount. Contact support@unbind.law for details.

**What happens to my data if I cancel?** Your data is retained for 90 days after cancellation. You can export all case data before or after canceling.

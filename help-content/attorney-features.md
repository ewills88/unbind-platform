# Unbind Platform Features for Attorneys

## AI Document Categorization
Unbind uses AI to automatically categorize uploaded documents into types like financial, legal, property, custody, tax, employment, and court documents. When a document is uploaded, the AI analyzes the filename and content to suggest a category with a confidence score. Attorneys can accept the AI suggestion or manually override the category. You can also click "AI Suggest" in the document preview to re-classify any document at any time.

## Case Management
Create and manage divorce cases with full lifecycle tracking. Each case includes:
- Client and spouse information
- Case status tracking (active, pending, closed)
- Timeline with events and deadlines
- Task management with assignments and due dates
- Financial tracking (assets, debts, income)
- Document management with sharing controls
- Real-time messaging with clients

## Discovery Tracking
Manage all discovery requests in one place:
- Create and track interrogatories, requests for production (RFP), requests for admission (RFA), subpoenas, and deposition notices
- Automatic deadline calculation based on state-specific rules and service method
- Urgency visualization (normal, warning, urgent, overdue)
- Extension request and grant workflow
- Generate discovery response documents as Word files
- Item-by-item response tracking with objection templates

## Settlement Tools
Comprehensive settlement negotiation suite:
- Create settlement proposals with property division, spousal support, child support, and custody terms
- Compare proposals side-by-side
- Counter-offer workflow
- Scenario modeler for alternative allocations
- Negotiation timeline tracking
- Mediation session manager
- Generate Marital Settlement Agreements (MSA) with template variables
- Signature workflow with status tracking (pending, sent, viewed, signed)
- Settlement analytics dashboard

## Billing & Invoicing
Full billing management:
- Time entry tracking with quick-time buttons and 6-minute increment rounding
- Activity type categorization (client communication, document review, court appearance, etc.)
- Invoice generation from unbilled time entries
- Payment tracking and processing via Stripe
- Trust account management with IOLTA compliance
- Expense tracking
- A/R aging reports
- QuickBooks export (CSV, IIF, QBO formats)

## Trust Accounting
IOLTA-compliant trust account management:
- Trust account ledger with running balances
- Deposit and withdrawal tracking
- Three-way reconciliation
- Client-level trust balances
- Audit trail for all transactions

## E-Filing
Submit court filings electronically through Tyler Technologies/Odyssey:
- Secure credential management with encryption
- Court location and filing code lookups
- Filing fee calculation
- Document bundling for submission
- Real-time status tracking (submitted, processing, accepted, rejected)
- Automatic stamped document retrieval
- Webhook notifications for status updates

## Client Portal
Give your clients secure access to their case:
- Magic link authentication (no password needed)
- Case status and progress tracking
- Secure messaging
- Document viewing and upload
- Appointment scheduling
- Online invoice payments
- Mobile-friendly PWA (installable on phone)

## Analytics & Reporting
Data-driven practice management:
- Revenue reports by month, attorney, case type
- A/R aging reports
- Attorney performance metrics (utilization, billable hours, settlement rates)
- Case lifecycle and outcomes analytics
- Export to PDF, Excel, or CSV
- Scheduled report delivery via email

## Supported States
Unbind supports all 50 US states with:
- State-specific residency requirements
- Waiting period calculations
- Property division rules (community property vs. equitable distribution)
- Custody standards
- Child support guidelines
- Filing fees and e-filing availability
- Detailed form libraries for California, Texas, and Florida with more states being added

## Integrations
- Google Calendar sync (bidirectional)
- Email notifications via Resend
- Webhook system for custom integrations
- QuickBooks export
- Stripe payment processing

# Client Portal User Guide

## How to Log In
Your attorney will send you an email with a login link (called a "magic link"). Here's how it works:
1. Check your email for a message from Unbind
2. Click the "Log In" button in the email
3. You'll be taken directly to your portal dashboard — no password needed!
4. The link expires after 15 minutes, so click it soon after receiving it
5. If the link expires, go to the portal login page and request a new one by entering your email

You can also set up a password if you prefer traditional login. Just toggle to "Password" on the login page.

## Viewing Your Case Status
When you log in, your dashboard shows:
- **Case progress** — A visual tracker showing what stage your case is at
- **Progress percentage** — How far along your case is overall
- **Recent activity** — What's happened recently (new documents, messages, status changes)
- **Quick actions** — Shortcuts to common tasks

## How to Upload Documents
Your attorney may request documents from you (like financial statements, tax returns, etc.):
1. Go to **Documents** in the portal menu
2. Look for "Document Requests" — these are documents your attorney needs from you
3. Click on a request and upload the file
4. The status will change to "Submitted" once uploaded
5. Your attorney will review and approve or request changes

You can also view and download any documents your attorney has shared with you.

## How to Download Shared Files
1. Go to **Documents** in the portal menu
2. You'll see all documents your attorney has shared with you
3. Click the download button next to any document to save it to your device
4. Use the search bar to find specific documents by name

## How to Message Your Attorney
Secure messaging is built right into the portal:
1. Go to **Messages** in the portal menu
2. Click **"New Conversation"** to start a new topic
3. Choose a category (general, document question, billing, scheduling, urgent)
4. Type your message and click Send
5. Your attorney will be notified and respond through the same thread
6. You'll see a notification when they reply

**Tips:**
- Press Enter to send, Shift+Enter for a new line
- Messages are secure and encrypted
- You can have multiple conversations open at once
- Use "Quick Replies" templates if available

## How to Pay Your Invoice
1. Go to **Billing** in the portal menu
2. You'll see a list of invoices from your attorney
3. Click **"Pay"** next to an outstanding invoice
4. Enter or select a payment method (credit card)
5. Confirm the amount and submit payment
6. You'll receive a confirmation, and the invoice will show as "Paid"

To manage payment methods:
- Click "Add Payment Method" to add a new card
- Set a default payment method for future payments
- View your payment history at any time

## Scheduling Appointments
1. Go to **Appointments** in the portal menu
2. Click **"Book Appointment"**
3. Step 1: Choose the type (consultation, document review, etc.) and format (in-person, video, phone)
4. Step 2: Pick an available date and time from the calendar
5. Step 3: Review and confirm
6. You'll see the appointment in your upcoming list

To cancel an appointment, click on it and select "Cancel" — you'll be asked for a reason.

## Completing Tasks
Your attorney may assign tasks to you:
1. Go to **Tasks** in the portal menu
2. You'll see tasks with their status, priority, and due date
3. Click "Start" to begin working on a task
4. When finished, click "Submit" and add any notes
5. Your attorney will review your submission

## Setting Up on Mobile (PWA)
You can install Unbind on your phone like an app:
1. Open your portal in your phone's browser (Chrome or Safari)
2. On Chrome: Tap the menu (three dots) → "Add to Home Screen"
3. On Safari: Tap the Share button → "Add to Home Screen"
4. The Unbind icon will appear on your home screen
5. Open it anytime for quick access — it works just like an app!

# Getting Started with Unbind — Attorney Onboarding

## Step 1: Set Up Your Firm Profile
After signing up, go to **Dashboard → Admin → Firm Settings** to configure:
- Firm name, address, phone, and website
- Firm logo (displayed in client portal and documents)
- Timezone and default settings
- Billing defaults (hourly rate, billing increment, payment terms)

## Step 2: Invite Team Members
Go to **Dashboard → Admin → User Management**:
1. Click "Invite User"
2. Enter their name, email, and role (attorney, paralegal, admin)
3. They'll receive an email invitation to create their account
4. You can manage roles and permissions under Roles & Permissions

## Step 3: Configure Billing Rates
Go to **Dashboard → Settings** or **Admin → Firm Settings → Billing**:
- Set your default hourly rate
- Configure minimum billing increment (e.g., 6 minutes)
- Set up retainer policies
- Configure payment terms (due on receipt, net 15, net 30)
- Connect Stripe for client payments

## Step 4: Create Your First Case
From the Dashboard, click **"New Case"**:
1. Enter client name and spouse name
2. Select your state
3. Choose case type (standard divorce, high-asset divorce, etc.)
4. The system generates a case number automatically
5. You'll see the case dashboard with tabs for overview, documents, messages, discovery, settlement, billing, and more

## Step 5: Set Up the Client Portal
For each case, you can invite your client to the portal:
1. Go to the case and find the client portal settings
2. Send a magic link invitation to the client's email
3. The client can then access their portal to view case updates, message you, upload documents, and pay invoices
4. No password needed — they log in via magic link every time

## Step 6: Upload Documents
Go to **Dashboard → Documents** or the case's Documents tab:
- Drag and drop files to upload
- AI will suggest a category for each document
- Share documents with clients by toggling the share option
- Tag documents for organization

## Step 7: Connect Google Calendar
Go to **Dashboard → Settings → Integrations**:
1. Click "Connect Google Calendar"
2. Authorize with your Google account
3. Select which calendar to sync
4. Case events and deadlines will sync to your calendar

## Step 8: Start Using Discovery & Settlement Tools
When your case progresses:
- Use the **Discovery tab** to create and track interrogatories, RFPs, and RFAs
- Use the **Settlement tab** to create proposals, compare terms, and generate MSAs
- Use the **Billing tab** to track time, generate invoices, and manage trust accounts

## Tips for Success
- **Use time tracking consistently** — Log time entries as you work to ensure accurate billing
- **Share key documents with clients** — They'll see shared documents in their portal
- **Set deadlines** — The system will remind you as they approach
- **Check analytics monthly** — Revenue and utilization reports help you manage your practice

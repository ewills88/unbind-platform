# Client Frequently Asked Questions

## What is Unbind?
Unbind is a secure online portal that your divorce attorney uses to manage your case. Through the portal, you can:
- See updates on your case status and progress
- Send and receive messages with your attorney
- View and download important documents
- Upload documents your attorney requests
- Pay invoices online
- Schedule appointments

Think of it as a secure hub for everything related to your case.

## Is Unbind Secure?
Yes. Unbind takes security very seriously:
- All data is encrypted in transit and at rest
- Messages between you and your attorney are secure
- Your login uses magic links (one-time-use links sent to your email) so there's no password to steal
- Only you and your attorney's team can see your case information
- Unbind complies with legal industry security standards

## What Can I See About My Case?
You can see:
- **Case progress** — What stage your case is at (filing, discovery, negotiation, etc.)
- **Documents** — Any files your attorney has shared with you
- **Messages** — All conversations with your attorney
- **Tasks** — Things your attorney needs you to do
- **Invoices** — Bills and payment history
- **Appointments** — Upcoming and past meetings

You will NOT see:
- Your attorney's internal notes or strategy
- Communications between attorneys
- Privileged work product
- Internal case management details

## How Do I Contact My Attorney?
The best way is through the **Messages** section in your portal:
1. Go to Messages
2. Start a new conversation or reply to an existing one
3. Your attorney will be notified immediately

For urgent matters, your attorney may have also provided a phone number or email outside of Unbind.

## What If I Have Trouble Logging In?
Try these steps:
1. **Check your email** — Look for the magic link email (check spam/junk folders too)
2. **Request a new link** — Go to the login page, enter your email, and click "Send Magic Link"
3. **Check the link hasn't expired** — Magic links expire after 15 minutes
4. **Try a different browser** — Sometimes clearing your browser or using a different one helps
5. **Contact support** — If nothing works, email support@unbind.law with your name and email address

## Can I Use Unbind on My Phone?
Yes! Unbind works great on mobile devices:
- Open it in your phone's browser (Chrome on Android, Safari on iPhone)
- You can also install it as an app on your home screen (see "Setting Up on Mobile" in the portal guide)
- All features work on mobile — messaging, documents, payments, everything

## Do I Need to Download an App?
No app download is required. Unbind works in your web browser. However, you CAN add it to your home screen as a Progressive Web App (PWA) for a more app-like experience. See the portal guide for instructions.

## How Much Does Unbind Cost Me?
Unbind is provided by your attorney as part of their practice management. There is no cost to you as a client for using the portal. You may see invoices from your attorney for their legal services, which you can pay through the portal.

## Can My Spouse See My Information?
No. Your portal access is private to you. Your spouse cannot see your messages, documents, or case details unless your attorney specifically shares something that both parties should see. Each party has their own separate, secure access.

## What Happens After My Case is Closed?
After your case is resolved:
- You'll still have access to your portal for a period of time to download documents
- Your attorney will let you know when access will end
- Make sure to download any important documents before access expires
- Payment history and receipts remain available

## Who Do I Contact for Help?
- **Case questions** → Message your attorney through the portal
- **Technical issues** → Email support@unbind.law
- **Billing questions** → Message your attorney or check the Billing section
